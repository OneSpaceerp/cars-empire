1. Product Strategy
1.1 Product Vision
Cars Empire is a vertical marketplace for automotive services. It combines local discovery, promotional offers, appointment booking, merchant operations, payment, digital vouchers, service execution, reviews, loyalty and post-service support in one system.
Core proposition:
DISCOVER → COMPARE → SELECT CAR → SELECT SERVICE → CHOOSE BRANCH / TIME → PAY → RECEIVE BOOKING/VOUCHER → ARRIVE/GET SERVICE → REDEEM/COMPLETE WORK ORDER → REVIEW → RETURN
1.2 In Scope
Periodic and on-demand car maintenance services.
Oil and filter change, brakes, tires, batteries, AC, detailing, car wash, diagnostics, inspections, alignment, balancing and electrical services.
Vehicle profiles and service history.
Branch-based and mobile/at-home service models.
Fixed-price deals and appointment-based services.
Merchant portals and technician/staff redemption tools.
Payments, deposits, full payments, wallets/cashback and refunds.
QR/code-based redemption and work-order closure.
Merchant settlements and commission accounting.
Location-aware search, maps, branch directions and service radius.
Reviews, favorites, notifications, support and loyalty.
1.3 Out of Scope for MVP
Full spare-parts ecommerce with warehouse management.
Complex insurance claim processing.
Full workshop ERP/accounting replacement.
Vehicle financing.
Predictive maintenance requiring IoT/OBD hardware.
Open public developer marketplace.

2.15 Advertising & Sponsored Placement Engine
Cars Empire shall include a global, revenue-generating advertising layer for merchants. The advertising layer is separate from ordinary marketplace ranking and is controlled by an ad-inventory service so that a merchant can rent a defined placement for a scheduled campaign.
Primary placement: a responsive sponsored banner/card similar to the benchmark reference supplied by the product owner. The placement is rendered by the consumer application and consumer website shell, not hard-coded into each page.
GLOBAL PLACEMENT RULE
Create a global consumer-facing placement slot named GLOBAL_TOP_BANNER. The slot must be available on every consumer app screen and every consumer website page that uses the standard Cars Empire shell.
The ad slot is an independent component placed after the global header/search area and before the page-specific content. The exact position may be configured by platform version, but the slot ID remains stable for reporting and billing.
If an eligible active campaign exists, render the best eligible creative. If no eligible campaign exists, the component must not render and must reserve ZERO vertical space; the page must behave exactly as if the slot does not exist.
The slot must not display on merchant, technician, admin or finance portals unless a separate B2B advertising product is introduced later.
The same slot must support app and web responsive creatives and must preserve safe-area, RTL/LTR and accessibility requirements.
MERCHANT ADVERTISING PRODUCT
Merchants can rent the placement for a defined date/time range, budget and targeting configuration.
Campaign must pass Cars Empire moderation/approval before becoming eligible to serve.
Merchant can choose a destination: offer detail, merchant page, service category, branch, campaign landing page or external URL if permitted by policy.
Campaign pricing must be configurable by Admin and support fixed daily placement, fixed campaign price, CPM, CPC and optional performance packages.
Campaigns require a billing owner, invoice/receipt reference, spend cap and payment/settlement state.
A campaign can be paused manually by merchant or Admin, or automatically when budget, schedule, inventory, policy or payment conditions fail.
TARGETING & ELIGIBILITY
Location: country, city, area, radius around a branch, or selected branch IDs.
Automotive category: maintenance, detailing, tires, batteries, AC, diagnostics, body care, inspection, etc.
Vehicle attributes: make, model family, year range, fuel type and other non-sensitive vehicle characteristics.
Audience: new users, returning users, high-frequency users, dormant users, customer segment, or all eligible users.
Time: day of week, hour range, campaign date range and optional blackout periods.
Context: page type, search category and selected vehicle/service intent.
Frequency cap: maximum impressions per user/day/campaign to prevent fatigue.
CREATIVE REQUIREMENTS
Required: desktop/mobile image asset, campaign title, short promotional message, CTA label, destination and sponsor/merchant identity.
Optional: discount badge, price, validity text, branch/location label and secondary label such as "Sponsored".
Recommended mobile creative ratio: approximately 1.5:1 to suit the supplied reference; the ad service may support additional responsive ratios for web.
Images must be compressed, served through CDN and have an accessible alt text.
The creative must display as sponsored/promotional content according to platform policy and must never be mistaken for an organic ranking result.
AD DELIVERY BEHAVIOR
Page Load → Request ad decision → Validate placement + targeting + schedule + budget + frequency cap → Return campaign or NO_AD
NO_AD → Do not render slot → Do not reserve layout height → Continue normal page layout
AD_FOUND → Render responsive creative → Track impression once viewability threshold is reached → Track click → Continue destination flow
The frontend must treat NO_AD as a normal successful response, not an error. Recommended API behavior is HTTP 200 with {"ad": null, "slot":"GLOBAL_TOP_BANNER"} for cached decisioning, while 204 may be supported internally at the edge if the client contract is kept equivalent.
AD BILLING & MEASUREMENT
Record impressions, viewable impressions, clicks, CTR, unique reach, frequency, spend, budget remaining, attributed offer views and optional attributed purchases.
Use a unique impression event ID and click event ID to make tracking idempotent.
Do not count duplicate client retries as additional billable impressions or clicks.
For CPC/CPM campaigns, billing events must be server-authoritative or signed before charging campaign budget.
Provide merchant reporting and Admin reporting by campaign, slot, date, city, category and branch.
AD INVENTORY COMMERCIAL RULES
Only one primary creative should occupy GLOBAL_TOP_BANNER at a given viewport position at one moment. Multiple eligible campaigns are ranked by campaign priority, targeting score, contract level, remaining budget and rotation rules.
Admin may define premium priority tiers, e.g. Standard, Featured and Exclusive.
Exclusive campaigns can reserve the slot for a merchant/campaign during a date range, blocking lower-priority campaigns.
If an exclusive campaign is active but has no deliverable creative due to a technical failure, the slot must fall back to the configured safe state and must not show stale/expired creative.

8.6 Advertising UX
The sponsored banner must feel native to the Cars Empire visual language but remain clearly distinguishable as a sponsored placement.
On mobile, use a wide card/banner with rounded corners, strong offer value, merchant identity and a single primary CTA. Avoid tiny text that cannot be read at normal viewing distance.
On web, support responsive widths and retina/high-density assets without changing the slot contract.
When no advertisement is available, remove the component entirely; no blank card, placeholder, reserved margin or broken image may remain.
If an ad is loading, use a lightweight non-layout-shifting placeholder only when necessary; after NO_AD the placeholder must collapse completely.
The ad destination must open in the same app/web context whenever possible and preserve campaign attribution through the subsequent offer/order flow.
Add an optional "Sponsored" label and merchant logo. Never label an organic offer as sponsored.
Respect dark mode, RTL Arabic layouts, safe-area insets and accessibility contrast.
Advertising-specific non-functional requirements:
Ad decision API p95 target: <= 150 ms from cache for standard traffic.
No ad response may block primary page rendering; fetch ads asynchronously when technically appropriate.
CDN cache creatives aggressively while keeping campaign eligibility server-controlled.
Ad delivery must remain available even if analytics collection is temporarily degraded; tracking must fail open for page rendering.
Campaign budget and billing mutations must be strongly transactional and idempotent.

10. Security, Privacy & Fraud Controls
Encrypt all traffic with HTTPS/TLS.
Hash passwords with Argon2id; never log passwords or OTPs.
Tokenize payment instruments through PSPs; do not store sensitive card data.
Use signed webhooks and idempotency keys for every payment event.
Rate-limit OTP, login, voucher validation and redemption endpoints.
Use short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens.
Add merchant-device binding or MFA for high-risk actions.
Enforce branch scope and staff scope server-side.
Protect against double redemption through row-level locking/transactional constraints.
Use fraud rules for repeated redemption, abnormal refunds, device/account clusters and suspicious merchant behavior.

17. Merchant Mobile App & Unique Coupon Redemption Engine
The Cars Empire merchant experience must include a dedicated mobile application for merchant owners, managers and staff/cashiers. Its highest-priority operational function is secure validation and redemption of customer coupon codes. Each purchased coupon must be an individual, one-time entitlement with a globally unique code and redemption token, even when multiple customers purchase the same deal.
17.1 Business Rules for Coupons
Every purchased coupon instance is unique. If a customer purchases quantity 3, the system creates 3 different coupon records and 3 different redeemable codes.
A deal/offer is a commercial template; a coupon is a unique customer entitlement generated from that template after successful payment.
Coupon codes are globally unique across the entire Cars Empire platform, not merely unique within a merchant or offer.
The QR payload/token is also globally unique and must not be reused, even after expiration or cancellation.
A coupon is single-use unless the offer explicitly supports controlled partial redemption. For standard automotive services, the default is one coupon -> one service redemption.
Redemption must be atomic and concurrency-safe. Two merchant devices must never be able to redeem the same coupon successfully.
Merchant staff can validate a code before redeeming it. Validation is read-only and does not consume the coupon.
Redemption requires an explicit confirmation action by the authorized merchant staff member.
A redeemed coupon creates or links to exactly one primary work order/service entitlement.
Coupons must remain available in the customer app after purchase, including an offline-readable voucher screen. Offline merchant redemption is permitted only where the selected operating policy supports signed offline tokens and later synchronization.
17.2 Coupon Code Generation
Recommended code design: a human-readable code plus a separate opaque QR token. Do not use sequential order numbers, database IDs or predictable timestamps as redeemable secrets.

17.7 Merchant Coupon Validation Flow
Merchant staff logs in and is bound to an authorized merchant branch.
Staff scans the customer QR code or enters the unique coupon code.
App sends the token/code plus branch/device context to the validation API.
Server verifies code/token authenticity, status, validity dates, merchant ownership, branch scope, booking requirement, vehicle eligibility and redemption policy.
Server returns a non-destructive VALID or INVALID result with the minimum operational information needed by staff.
Staff reviews the details with the customer and presses CONFIRM REDEMPTION.
Server acquires a row-level lock or equivalent atomic claim on the coupon, re-checks all conditions, creates the redemption, marks the coupon REDEEMED and creates/links the work order in one transaction.
App receives success and displays the redemption/work-order reference.
Customer receives a redemption notification and the service becomes visible in service history.
17.8 Redemption State Machine
Coupon entitlement state:
CREATED -> AVAILABLE -> VALIDATED -> REDEEMING -> REDEEMED -> SERVICE_COMPLETED
Alternative terminal/exception paths:
AVAILABLE -> EXPIRED
AVAILABLE -> CANCELLED
AVAILABLE -> REFUNDED
AVAILABLE -> SUSPENDED
REDEEMED -> REVERSED only through a privileged, reason-coded support/admin process
REDEEMED -> DISPUTED when a merchant/customer case requires investigation; this does not automatically make the coupon reusable
Validation itself must not change AVAILABLE to REDEEMED. The state transition occurs only on the authenticated redemption command.

17.10 Concurrency & Double-Redemption Protection
The redemption endpoint must use a transaction with SELECT ... FOR UPDATE, an atomic compare-and-set, or an equivalent distributed lock strategy.
Before creating the redemption, the service must re-read the coupon and validate that status=AVAILABLE (or the approved pre-redeem state).
Only one redemption may win when two devices submit the same coupon concurrently.
The losing request receives a deterministic ALREADY_REDEEMED response and never creates a second work order.
Idempotency-Key is required for the redeem command. Repeating the same command with the same key returns the original successful response.
A different idempotency key against an already-redeemed coupon must return an entitlement-state error, not another redemption.
17.11 Unique Coupon Generation Pseudocode
Pseudocode:
FOR each paid quantity unit:  LOOP:    random_part = CSPRNG(10-14 characters)    candidate = normalized(prefix + random_part)    IF NOT exists(coupon_code_normalized=candidate):       INSERT coupon with unique constraint       BREAK    ELSE retry  generate independent qr_token with CSPRNGCOMMIT
The database UNIQUE constraint is authoritative. Application-level existence checks are only an optimization and must not be treated as the final collision guarantee.

17.13 Security Requirements for Merchant Redemption
Every merchant device receives a registered device identity and is associated with a merchant/branch scope.
A cashier cannot redeem coupons belonging to another merchant.
Branch scope is checked server-side; the client must not be trusted to select an arbitrary branch.
The app should support PIN/biometric re-entry for fast counter operations without keeping long-lived unrestricted sessions.
Failed code attempts are rate-limited and logged for abuse detection.
After repeated invalid-code attempts, impose progressive throttling and optionally require re-authentication.
Never expose full payment card information, customer phone number or unnecessary personal information to cashiers.
All redemption actions store actor, device, branch, timestamp and reason metadata.
Admin/support reversal of a redeemed coupon must require a reason code and audit trail.
Screenshots or clipboard export of coupon codes should be handled according to the chosen threat model; the server remains the source of truth.
17.14 Merchant App Offline Strategy
Recommended operating mode: online-by-default. Offline redemption is an optional controlled capability, not the default. If enabled, the merchant app receives signed, short-lived validation bundles for the branch. Offline redemption events are stored with monotonic sequence numbers, cryptographic signatures and device identity, then synchronized when connectivity returns. The server reconciles the event and rejects any conflicting or replayed redemption.
17.15 Unique Coupon Lifecycle Example
Example: the customer purchases 3 units of a "Full Synthetic Oil Change" deal.
Order quantity = 3.
Payment captured once for the order.
System generates Coupon A, Coupon B and Coupon C as three separate rows.
Coupon A = CE-OIL-7K4P9Q2M; Coupon B = CE-OIL-4N8X2T6P; Coupon C = CE-OIL-9R3L5W1K.
Each has a unique QR token and independent redemption status.
When Coupon B is redeemed, A and C remain AVAILABLE.
If B is refunded before redemption, B moves to REFUNDED and cannot be redeemed.
If B has already been redeemed, a later refund is handled by the refund/dispute policy and does not silently recreate an AVAILABLE coupon.

17.18 Implementation Recommendation
Treat the unique coupon service as a dedicated domain module rather than embedding redemption logic inside the merchant UI or general order service. The module owns issuance, uniqueness, validation, redemption, reversal, audit events and synchronization. The merchant app is a thin operational client over this authoritative service. This separation is essential for preventing duplicate redemption, enforcing merchant scope and supporting future channels such as web merchant portal, POS integration, partner APIs and kiosk redemption.
Cars Empire Product Rule: Every purchased service entitlement is a unique coupon. Every unique coupon can be redeemed at most once unless an explicit multi-redemption policy is configured for that offer.
Implementation Clarification: The Merchant App is an operational application for merchant owners/managers/cashiers, and the coupon engine guarantees one unique redeemable coupon code per purchased service unit. A deal does not have one reusable code; every sold unit receives a different code.
